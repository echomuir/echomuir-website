General Questions

Do Echomuir apps work offline?
Yes. All Echomuir apps are designed to work offline. No internet connection required once installed.
What devices are supported?
All apps are built to work on modern iOS devices without lag or compatibility issues. See each app's support page for specific iOS version requirements.
Do Echomuir apps collect personal data?
No. Echomuir apps don't collect, store, transmit, or share personal data. There are no accounts, no analytics, and no advertising. See each app's privacy policy for details.
